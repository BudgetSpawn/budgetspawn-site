Sinking funds, done properly

Money you'll need later, put away on purpose… now!

New Tyres in 3 yrs from now. Annual school fees in January. That holiday you keep postponing. From a few simple prompts, BudgetSpawn works out what each one costs you per PAYDAY cycle (Daily, Weekly, Monthly), then keeps count — so the bill arrives and the money is already there. And you remain in control of your MONEY and can even withdraw or loan against it, intelligently! All in the palm of your hand, literally… no more guesswork!!!

In testing. Android first, iOS to follow.

How it works

Spawn a goal

Say what it costs and when you need it. BudgetSpawn allows for inflation and divides the rest across your paydays — weekly, fortnightly, monthly, whatever you're actually paid.

Inject on payday

Put in what you can spare. It's split across your goals, oldest debt first, and the split always adds back to the exact amount you entered.

Borrow from yourself

Short this month? Take a loan against your own savings instead of a lender. Interest is charged deliberately — and it comes back to you, not to anyone else.

Your money stays on your phone.

BudgetSpawn is not a bank and it isn't connected to one. There is no account to create, no password, no server holding your figures. The app has no internet permission at all — it cannot send your data anywhere, because it cannot reach anywhere.

That's a design decision, not a limitation. Read the privacy policy for the full detail.
